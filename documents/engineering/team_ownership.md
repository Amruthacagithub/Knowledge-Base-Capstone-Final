# Team Ownership — Engineering

## Platform Services

- **Platform Team** owns and maintains **API Gateway** and **Billing Service**.
- **Identity Team** owns **User Service** and **Auth Service**.
- **Search Team** owns **Document Service** and **Search Indexer**.
- **SRE Team** owns **PostgreSQL Database** operations and **Redis Cache** clusters.

## Incident Ownership

- **SRE Team** is the primary owner for **INC-5041** remediation follow-up.
- **Platform Team** owns payment-path incidents involving **Stripe Gateway**.

## Escalation

Ownership disputes escalate to the VP Engineering within one business day.
