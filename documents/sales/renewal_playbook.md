# Renewal Playbook — TechNova Sales

## Timeline (90 days before renewal)

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| T-90 | CSM sends health check survey; review product usage metrics |
| T-60 | QBR scheduled; identify expansion opportunities |
| T-45 | Renewal quote prepared; early renewal discount eligible (5%) |
| T-30 | Executive sponsor outreach if red account |
| T-14 | Legal sends renewal order form |
| T-7 | Escalate if unsigned; VP Sales involvement |
| T-0 | Auto-renew or churn process |

## Health Scoring

Green: DAU/MAU > 40%, 3+ departments active, NPS > 8.  
Yellow: declining usage or open P1 tickets.  
Red: champion left, no login 30 days — assign save team.

## Expansion Plays

- Add departments (HR → Eng → Sales)
- Upgrade tier for SSO or VPC
- Add professional services for new data sources

## Churn Prevention

Common churn reasons: poor ingestion quality, permission misconfiguration, lack of training. Mitigate with 30-day success plan and SE office hours.

## Pricing on Renewal

Standard uplift **3–5%** unless multi-year lock. Match competitor only with competitive intel form approved.

## Handoff

Renewals owned by AE of record; CSM supports. Commission: 50% of renewal ARR at quota credit.
