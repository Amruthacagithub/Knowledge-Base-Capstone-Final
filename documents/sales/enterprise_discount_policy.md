# Enterprise Discount Policy — Sales

## Standard Discounts

- **Enterprise** tier: custom pricing; typical contract discount **25%** off list.
- Multi-year deals: additional **5%** per committed year (max 15%).

## Approval Matrix

| Discount | Approver |
|----------|----------|
| Up to 20% | Account Executive |
| 21–30% | Sales Director |
| Above 30% | VP Sales |

## Restricted Information

Internal **Stripe Gateway** fee schedules and platform cost models are Engineering-confidential and must not be shared with prospects.

## CRM

All approved discounts must be recorded in Salesforce before quote generation.
