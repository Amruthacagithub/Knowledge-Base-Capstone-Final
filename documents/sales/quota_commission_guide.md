# Quota and Commission Guide — TechNova Sales (Restricted)

## Annual Quota

| Role | Typical quota (ARR) |
|------|---------------------|
| SMB AE | $600k |
| Mid-Market AE | $1.2M |
| Enterprise AE | $2.0M |

Quotas set in January; mid-year adjustments rare and VP-approved only.

## Commission Structure

- **Base / variable:** 50/50 split typical for AEs.
- **Accelerator:** 1.5× rate on attainment above 100% quota.
- **SPIFFs:** Quarterly on strategic products (Enterprise VPC, new logo in FinServ).

## Commission Schedule

| Event | Payout |
|-------|--------|
| New logo closed | 50% at booking, 50% at collected payment |
| Renewal | 50% of renewal commission rate (see renewal_playbook) |
| Clawback | If churn < 12 months, prorated clawback applies |

## Sales Commission Structure (FAQ)

**Q: What is the sales commission structure?**  
A: AEs earn variable comp as % of ARR booked per tier: SMB 12%, Mid-Market 10%, Enterprise 8% of first-year ACV, plus accelerators above quota. BDRs earn quarterly bonus on qualified meetings that close within 6 months.

**Q: When are commissions paid?**  
A: Monthly payroll following Finance collection verification.

## Team Overrides

Sales Director override: 2% of team bookings. SE not on commission; bonus pool separate.

## Disputes

Submit commission disputes to Sales Ops within 30 days of statement.
