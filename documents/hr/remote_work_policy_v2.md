# Remote Work Policy — Version 2

**Effective:** 2024-07-01  
**Status:** Current

## Eligibility

All full-time employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week** with manager approval.

## Equipment

Company provides a laptop and a **$500 annual home office stipend**.

## Core Hours

Employees must be available 10:00–15:00 in their local timezone.

## Change from v1

Remote allowance increased from 2 to 3 days per week. Home office stipend introduced.
