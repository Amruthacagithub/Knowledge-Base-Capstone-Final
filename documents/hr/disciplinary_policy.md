# Disciplinary Policy — TechNova Inc.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors working on TechNova premises or systems. It describes progressive discipline for performance and conduct issues.

## Principles

- **Fairness:** Employees receive clear expectations and opportunity to improve.
- **Consistency:** Similar violations receive similar outcomes across departments.
- **Documentation:** All steps are recorded in Workday with manager and HR notes.
- **Confidentiality:** Disciplinary records are restricted to HR, leadership, and direct management chain.

## Progressive Discipline Steps

### Step 1 — Verbal Counseling

Used for minor first-time issues (tardiness, missed 1:1, minor policy misunderstanding). Manager documents conversation within 48 hours. No formal file in permanent record unless repeated within 12 months.

### Step 2 — Written Warning

Issued when behavior continues after verbal counseling or for moderate violations (unapproved absence, inappropriate Slack conduct, repeated missed deadlines). Employee signs acknowledgment; copy to HR.

### Step 3 — Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

30–90 day PIP with measurable goals for sustained underperformance or serious conduct concerns. Weekly check-ins required. Failure to meet PIP may proceed to Step 4.

### Step 4 — Final Written Warning

Last step before termination for most cases. HR must approve. Employee may request peer support person in meeting (non-legal advocate).

### Step 5 — Termination

Immediate termination without prior steps may occur for: harassment, discrimination, theft, violence, intentional data breach, or gross insubordination. Legal and HR review required before action.

## Performance vs Conduct

Performance issues typically follow PIP path. Conduct violations may skip steps based on severity matrix in HR portal appendix.

## Appeals

Employees may appeal written warnings or PIP within **10 business days** to HR Business Partner. Appeals are reviewed by HR Director within 15 business days.

## Reinstatement

Successful completion of PIP removes active PIP status; prior warnings remain on file per retention schedule (3 years for warnings, 7 years for terminations).
