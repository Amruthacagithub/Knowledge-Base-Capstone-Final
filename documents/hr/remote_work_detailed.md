# Remote Work Policy (Detailed) — TechNova Inc.

## Policy Statement

TechNova supports a **hybrid work model** balancing collaboration in-office with flexibility for focused remote work. This document expands the Employee Handbook remote work section.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees after **90-day probation** are eligible unless role is designated **onsite-required** (facilities, hardware lab, executive assistant). Onsite-required list is maintained by HR and Engineering leadership.

## Standard Schedule

- **Up to 3 remote days per week** by default.
- **Core in-office days: Tuesday and Thursday** for all hybrid-eligible staff.
- Managers may approve temporary 4-day remote weeks for travel or medical accommodation.

## Core Hours

Remote and in-office employees must be reachable **10:00 AM – 4:00 PM** in their local time zone on working days. Meetings outside core hours require mutual agreement.

## Equipment and Security

- Company laptop required; personal devices may not access production systems.
- VPN mandatory for internal tools when off corporate network.
- Home network must use WPA2+ encryption; screen lock after 5 minutes idle.
- Confidential conversations require private space; no public café screens for restricted data.

## Expenses

Home internet stipend: **$75/month** via Expensify (see Benefits Guide). One-time **$300** home office setup allowance for new hires.

## International Remote Work

Work outside country of employment for more than **14 days per year** requires HR and Legal approval (tax and entity implications). Emergency travel exceptions: contact HR within 48 hours.

## Performance Expectations

Remote work is not a substitute for performance. Managers evaluate outcomes, not presence. Abuse of flexibility (unreachable, missed commitments) may revoke remote privileges.

## Revocation

HR may suspend remote work for policy violations or role changes with 2 weeks notice except for immediate security incidents.
