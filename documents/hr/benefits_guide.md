# Benefits Guide — TechNova Inc.

## Overview

TechNova Inc. provides competitive benefits to all full-time employees (30+ hours per week). This guide summarizes health, retirement, wellness, and ancillary programs. For enrollment deadlines and forms, use the HR portal.

## Health Insurance

TechNova offers three medical plan tiers through BlueCross:

- **Bronze** — Lower premium, higher deductible ($2,500 individual / $5,000 family).
- **Silver** — Balanced option; company contributes 80% of employee premium.
- **Gold** — Premium coverage; recommended for employees with dependents.

Dental and vision are included at no additional cost on Silver and Gold plans. Open enrollment runs each November for January 1 effective dates. Qualifying life events allow mid-year changes within 30 days.

## Retirement — 401(k)

Employees are eligible after 90 days of employment. TechNova matches **100% of the first 4%** and **50% of the next 2%** of salary deferred. Vesting schedule: 25% per year; fully vested after four years. Default investment is a target-date fund unless you elect otherwise.

## Wellness and Mental Health

- **Gym stipend:** $50/month with receipt upload.
- **Mental health:** 12 therapy sessions per year via Spring Health at no copay.
- **EAP:** 24/7 counseling and legal/financial referral line.

## Parental and Family Leave

Primary caregivers receive **12 weeks paid parental leave**. Secondary caregivers receive **4 weeks paid**. Adoption and foster placement qualify on the same terms after one year of tenure.

## Life and Disability

- Basic life insurance: 1× annual salary (company-paid).
- Supplemental life: optional employee-paid up to 5× salary.
- Short-term disability: 60% salary for up to 12 weeks.
- Long-term disability: 60% salary after 90-day elimination period.

## Commuter and Remote Work Stipend

Hybrid employees may claim **$75/month** for home internet or transit. Submit via Expensify under category `Benefits — Commuter`.

## Questions

Contact HR at hr@technova.com or Slack `#hr-benefits`. For urgent medical accommodation requests, email hr-accommodations@technova.com.
