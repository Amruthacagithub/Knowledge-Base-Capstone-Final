# Remote Work Policy — Version 1

**Effective:** 2023-01-01 to 2024-06-30  
**Status:** Superseded

## Eligibility

All full-time employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week** with manager approval.

## Equipment

Company provides a laptop only; no home office stipend in v1.

## Core Hours

Employees must be available 10:00–16:00 in their local timezone.

## Supersession

Replaced by Remote Work Policy v2 effective 2024-07-01.
