# Performance Review Process — TechNova Inc.

## Cycle Overview

TechNova conducts **biannual performance reviews** in June and December. Reviews inform compensation adjustments, promotion decisions, and development planning.

## Timeline

| Phase | June cycle | December cycle |
|-------|------------|----------------|
| Self-assessment opens | May 15 | Nov 15 |
| Manager reviews due | Jun 1 | Dec 1 |
| Calibration meetings | Jun 8–15 | Dec 8–15 |
| Feedback delivered | Jun 20 | Dec 20 |
| Compensation effective | Jul 1 | Jan 1 |

## Rating Scale

1. **Exceptional** — Consistently exceeds expectations; role model (top ~10%).
2. **Exceeds** — Frequently exceeds key results (~20%).
3. **Meets** — Delivers expected outcomes (~55%).
4. **Developing** — Partially meets; improvement plan recommended (~10%).
5. **Unsatisfactory** — Does not meet; PIP or separation considered (~5%).

Calibration ensures department ratings align with company distribution guidelines.

## Self-Assessment

Employees complete:

- Key accomplishments vs goals
- TechNova values demonstration (Collaboration, Customer Focus, Integrity, Innovation)
- Development goals for next cycle
- Optional peer feedback requests (2–3 peers)

## Manager Responsibilities

Managers must:

- Hold continuous feedback conversations (monthly 1:1 minimum)
- Document examples with dates and impact
- Avoid bias (recency, halo); use calibration rubric
- Complete reviews before calibration lock

## 360 Feedback (Senior Roles)

Director+ roles receive structured 360 feedback from peers, reports, and cross-functional partners. Results are shared only with employee and their manager.

## Outcomes

Reviews link to:

- Merit increase budget (0–8% typical band)
- Promotion nominations (separate committee in Q1/Q3)
- Learning stipend eligibility ($1,500/year for Meets+)
