# Leave Policy — TechNova Inc.

## Purpose

This policy defines paid time off (PTO), sick leave, bereavement, jury duty, and unpaid leave for all TechNova employees. It aligns with our Employee Handbook and state where applicable.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue **20 days PTO per calendar year** at **1.67 days per month**. Part-time employees (20–29 hours) accrue pro-rated at 0.83 days per month.

### Carryover and Expiration

- Maximum **5 unused PTO days** may carry to the next calendar year.
- Days beyond the carryover cap expire on **December 31**.
- PTO cannot be cashed out except where required by law.

### Request Process

1. Submit requests in the HR portal at least **14 days** in advance for blocks under 5 days.
2. Requests of **5+ consecutive days** require **30 days** notice.
3. Managers respond within **3 business days**.
4. During peak release windows (see Engineering calendar), Engineering managers may defer non-critical PTO with 48 hours notice and an alternative date.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 sick days per year**, separate from PTO. Sick leave does not carry over. For absences exceeding **3 consecutive days**, a healthcare provider note may be requested upon return.

## Bereavement Leave

- **5 paid days** for immediate family (spouse, parent, child, sibling).
- **3 paid days** for extended family (grandparent, in-law).
- Additional unpaid leave may be approved by HR.

## Jury Duty and Civic Leave

Jury duty is paid for up to **10 days** per year with summons documentation. Voting time: up to **4 hours** paid on election days.

## Unpaid Leave

Unpaid personal leave of 30+ days requires VP approval and is available after one year of tenure. Benefits continue for the first 30 days of approved unpaid leave.

## Holidays

TechNova observes 10 company holidays annually (list published each January in the HR portal). Holidays do not count against PTO.

## Tracking and Audits

All leave is tracked in Workday. Managers must approve before time off begins. HR audits quarterly for negative balances and policy exceptions.
